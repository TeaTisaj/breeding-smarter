## Results that contradict the expected story (reported, not tuned away)
1. **H3 fails in both breeds.** Mating method matters as much as contribution control.
   - Minimum-coancestry mating without quotas (CC-none-mc1, "each dam gets her least-related allowed sire") was the worst arm: ΔF 2.29% (SYN_S) and 0.89% (SYN_M), with Ne 22 and 56.
   - The top 5 sires got 61% of litters in both breeds.
   - Minimum-kinship mating is only harmless inside OCS quotas. MK exclusion and MK-scaled caps did not stop the concentration under MC1 (top-5 share 57 to 70% and 20 to 47%).
   - The literature result that mating design hardly changes asymptotic ΔF assumes contributions are fixed. Here, unconstrained mate choice changes the contributions. This is the P8 mechanism in its purest form.
2. **H5 fails in SYN_M.** OCS on true kinship reached Ne 763 vs 953 for random mating. The bug check prescribed by the plan found no bug (`scripts/check_h5_oracle.py`, a SYN_M season on true kinship):
   - equal contributions are feasible under the caps;
   - conservation optimum C = 0.1208 vs equal split 0.1287;
   - after Sainte-Laguë rounding 0.1209.
   Yearly OCS minimises the coancestry of each season's matings and gives litters to about 100 low-kinship sires; random use of all ~660 sires spreads contributions more evenly over the long run in this larger population. In SYN_S the oracle beats random mating threefold.
3. **The product mode S3q keeps the diversity gain but loses most of S3's health and HD benefits** (SYN_M, generation 10):

   | | S3q | S1 | S3 |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | MDR1 affected | 0.29% | 0.32% | 0.01% |
   | SOD1 affected | 0.39% | 0.25% | 0.10% |
   | Mean true HD breeding value | 0.40 | 0.39 | 0.06 |

   Quotas go to low-MK sires regardless of EBV or carrier status, and the breeder's top three come only from those sires.
4. **Random mating is still the best single arm** in SYN_M, and in SYN_S among domestic-sire arms.
5. **Recorded kinship still overstates the gain.**
   - Under CC-ocs-mc1 in SYN_M, recorded COI at generation 10 is 2.7% vs realised IBD 12.7%.
   - In SYN_S the gain arms never hit an infeasible season, because the budget is set on recorded kinship. Their true ΔF overshoots the lower targets: 0.25% target gives 0.75% realised; 0.5% gives 0.79%; 1% gives 1.04%.
   - In SYN_M the budgets were met in truth: targets 0.25, 0.5 and 1% gave 0.26, 0.45 and 0.86%.
6. **Conservation OCS shortens the generation interval**: SYN_M 4.5 years vs 5.2 under S1 and S0; SYN_S 4.8 vs 5.4. This is the effect Windig et al. warned about. Oracle and gain arms lengthen it (5.6 to 6.2).

## Additional analyses (not pre-registered)
- **Foreign sires explain CC-none-rand vs S0.** In SYN_S, CC-none-rand (random among rule-compliant domestic and registered foreign sires) has ΔF 0.93% vs S0 (domestic only) 0.67%.
  - Diagnostic (`scripts/diag_foreign_sires.py`, replicates 0 to 3): CC-none-rand with domestic sires only gives ΔF 0.49 to 0.75%, in line with S0 (0.55 to 1.11%). With foreign sires: 0.94 to 1.23%.
  - SYN_S's registered foreign males (19 of about 160 candidates) come from a small, inbred foreign population (mean true F 0.48 vs 0.43 domestic).
  - All P12 arms, S3 and S4 use them (decision D2, as in P8); S0 does not. Comparisons with S0 carry this confound.
- **Gain OCS under a tight budget dominates conservation OCS** on the HD trait (E6, SYN_M):

  | | CC-gain010-mc1 | CC-ocs-mc1 |
  |---|---|---|
  | late ΔF | 0.176% | 0.176% |
  | Ne | 288 | 289 |
  | Mean true HD breeding value at generation 10 (lower is better) | −0.79 | +0.39 |

  - Looser budgets buy more HD gain at a ΔF cost: 0.25% gives −0.96 at 0.26%; 0.5% gives −1.12 at 0.45%; 1% gives −1.35 at 0.86% (Fig I).
  - This suggests gain OCS with a small budget is the better default for the product mode. **S3q was not changed after the results** (Open questions).
- **Adoption with quotas (E7)** — late ΔF in % per generation:

  | breed | 0% | 25% | 50% | 75% | 100% |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | SYN_S | 1.59 | 1.66 | 0.95 | 0.85 | 1.02 |
  | SYN_M | 0.25 | 0.19 | 0.14 | 0.13 | 0.15 |

  Most of the benefit appears from 50% adoption, matching P8 E2's threshold. The benefit is much larger than without quotas (E2: 0.508 → 0.495 realised IBD).

## Deviations from the plan (and why)
1. **robustocs cross-check replaced by scipy SLSQP.** robustocs 0.2.1 requires `gurobipy` (a commercial licence). The robustocs test exists and skips; SLSQP plus closed-form cases are the independent check (ADR-007).
2. **E5 also runs S0** (the plan listed S1 and S3 as anchors). Fig G and H5 need S0, and the same seeds make it pairable. E5 and E6 rerun S1, S3 and CC-ocs-mc1 with the E1 seed instead of reusing E1 parts, because the new metrics M12 to M15 are needed.
3. **C_now** is the coancestry of last year's recorded domestic matings, recomputed with this year's kinship. That is D5 as written; the 12.8 text said "from its previous year's plan".
4. **`none × random` is not identical to S0.** It avoids HARD-rule pairs and includes registered foreign sires (D2). The second difference matters; see Additional analyses.
5. **Failed solves.** If OCS is infeasible with all constraints, the age-class constraint, then depth, are dropped and logged. This never happened (`relaxed_constraints` empty in all seasons).
6. **Overflow.** A dam whose allowed sires all lack quota gets her least-cost (MC1) or a random allowed sire, counted as overflow (D7_overflow). The contract checker allows exactly that.
7. **Club tool candidate sires** (CLI and app, not the experiments) are breeding-age males in use: sired registered offspring in 5 years or have an HD result. At most 1,500 are kept, lowest MK first. All registered males made SYN_L take 628 s per plan. The S3q product toggle in the app uses the conservation plan.
8. **Auto dams.** If more are eligible than last year's number of litters, a seeded random sample of that size is used (one season). Not specified in the plan; documented in `docs/coordination.md`.
9. **H5 uses Ne = 1/(2 × mean late ΔF)** per arm. Per-replicate Ne is infinite when a replicate's late ΔF is ≤ 0, which happens for S0 in SYN_M. The H1, H4b and H6 paired Ne differences use replicates with finite Ne; n is reported (all replicates were finite for those).
10. **Fig D** now uses the first and last generation present instead of fixed 1 and 10 (needed for short test runs; identical on 10-generation runs).
11. **Bundle figures.** When two experiments share a figure name (C, F), the first experiment listed keeps it, so E1's figures stay on the results page.
12. **Benchmark timing.** The `plan_season` benchmark first ran while 6 experiment workers were busy: 2.48 s, above the 2 s target. Rerun on the idle machine: 0.43 s.

## Open questions for Tea
- **Product default.** Should the club planner's default be gain OCS with a small ΔF budget instead of pure conservation? In SYN_M it gave the same Ne with far better hips. This would also likely restore S3q's HD benefit. It was not changed after the results, to respect pre-registration. A follow-up experiment (S3q on gain OCS) would test it.
- **Poster framing.** Suggestion: "Club quotas plus our recommender: Ne up from 33 to 54 (small breed), 188 to 328 (medium); random mating remains the diversity benchmark." The poster text now says this in shorter form.
- **Carrier status in quotas.** Should quotas take carrier status into account (H4a)? A linear constraint on the expected carrier frequency is easy to add (`docs/research_coordination.md` recommends health as linear constraints).
