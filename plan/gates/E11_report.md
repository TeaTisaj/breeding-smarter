## What it means
1. **The herding mechanism is robust.** In every population and at every breeder-freedom setting, the recommender alone concentrates sire use.
2. **The fix is robust, with one weak population.** Quotas beat the market in 3 of 4 populations. The full health arm (quotas + hips budget + carrier limit) beats the market on all three outcomes in 4 of 4, but in v1d its Ne interval only just excludes 0 (+35, +0.3 to +70), and the coancestry cross-check there includes 0 (+20, −6 to +46). The paper reports v1d as the weak case.
3. **OCS is needed; simple caps are not enough** when breeders choose with the recommender. Flat MK-scaled caps and MK exclusion lose about 140 to 150 Ne in SYN_M. This answers the reviewer question "why not a simple sire cap?".
4. **Partial adoption is no longer a limitation for the health goal.** Bounding only the adopters' litters keeps the limit active in every season, and at 50% participation alleles, hips and Ne all beat the market. With the old bound, 50% participation gives no allele gain (+0.1, −1.7 to +1.8), which reproduces H10-adopt.
5. **Small breed (SYN_S):** quotas beat the market on Ne in 3 of 4 populations (v1c +7.5, −0.2 to +15); OCS beats simple controls; herding (fewer sires) in 3 of 4 (not in v1c).

## Additional analysis (not pre-registered): does herding lower Ne?
The paper's earlier wording "the recommender lowers Ne" comes from E5's 10-generation trend (M3_ne). In E11, S3 − S1:

| population | M3_ne (10-generation trend) | late window |
|---|---|---|
| v1 (E11b, 20 reps) | −77 (−108 to −47) | not detectable |
| v1 (E11a, 10 reps) | −45 (−89 to 0) | +3 (−34 to +40) |
| v1b | −6 (−15 to +2) | −9 (−34 to +17) |
| v1c | −23 (−39 to −7) | −21 (−43 to +1) |
| v1d | −92 (−105 to −79) | −51 (−72 to −29) |

Same at every freedom setting (M3_ne −76 to −85 in E11b). Reading: the recommender **never improved** Ne, lowered it on the 10-generation measure in most populations, and the cost is concentrated in the early generations. **Paper wording must be "does not improve and in most populations lowers Ne", not "lowers Ne".**

## Deviations from the plan
1. **Threads and memory.** The first main run used 6 workers with default library threading; load reached 30 to 44 on 8 cores. It was stopped at 384 readable parts (0 corrupt) and resumed with single-threaded libraries (`OMP_NUM_THREADS=1` and related). Other applications filled memory (15 of 16 GB, 7 GB compressed) until they were closed. Results do not depend on thread count (seeded per replicate; determinism tests). Wall-clock 15:12 to 18:47.
2. **Display bug fixed after the first evaluation.** The pooled H11-pop-c row was labelled "Ne, HD, freq" and so printed as a percentage (8261 instead of 82.6). Label changed to "Ne late", test added (`test_e11_pooled_rows_are_not_shown_as_percent`). No value or pass changed.
3. **Solver warnings.** Clarabel printed "Solution may be inaccurate" 10 times over the whole run; such solutions are accepted as before (E5 to E10 behaviour).
