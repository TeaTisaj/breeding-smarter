# Datasheet: Breeding Smarter synthetic dog pedigree dataset (v1)

Following "Datasheets for Datasets" (Gebru et al.). Generated by `bsm export release`.

**All animals are synthetic. Any resemblance to real dogs, kennels or registration numbers is accidental.**

## Motivation
- **Why:** real dog pedigree data is not openly available (national registries such as SKK,
  KC and FKC do not publish full pedigrees with health and DNA data), so methods for pedigree
  analysis, mate matching and rule compliance cannot be benchmarked openly. This dataset
  provides a realistic, calibrated synthetic registry together with the ground truth that no
  real registry has (realised inbreeding, true breeding values, untested genotypes).
- **Who:** Tea Ćetojević Tisaj and TODO colleague, University of Zagreb, Faculty of Electrical
  Engineering and Computing (FER), for BioMedVetMech 2026 (Zagreb, 24 to 25 October 2026).
- **Funding:** none.

## Composition
- Three fictional breeds: SYN_L (large, popular), SYN_M (medium, strong popular-sire use),
  SYN_S (small local breed with a limited gene pool). Registered animals per breed:
  SYN_L 125,167, SYN_M 24,616, SYN_S 3,858; birth years 1934 to
  2026.
- One row of `registry/animals` is one registered dog (domestic registrations, imports and
  pedigree records of foreign ancestors); `registry/litters` one registered litter;
  `health_tests` one official HD screening; `dna_tests` one DNA test result; `ebv` one HD
  breeding value; `truth/animals_truth` one simulated animal including hidden ones;
  `truth/genotypes_truth` one animal x disease locus.

- **Observed vs truth.** `registry/` holds only what a kennel club would record. `truth/` holds
  the ground truth for benchmarking: every simulated animal (including the hidden pre-1980
  history and unrecorded foreign animals), true parents, realised identity by descent from a
  neutral gene drop, pedigree inbreeding on the full true pedigree, true HD breeding values and
  genotypes at all disease loci, including one deliberately untestable variant (`NOVEL_X`).
- **Identifiers are visibly synthetic:** registration numbers start with `SYN-`; microchip
  numbers use the ISO 11784 code 999, reserved for test transponders (ICAR); breed, kennel and
  dog names are generated from syllables.
- Field-level documentation: `data_dictionary.md`.

## Generation process
- Open, seeded, agent-based simulator (`breeding_smarter.sim`), one year per step from
  1935 to 2026: unrelated base founders, a hidden burn-in history, a
  domestic/foreign split, registry founding in 1980 (all living domestic dogs registered,
  adults with unknown parents), a status-quo breeding market with popular sires
  (rich-get-richer), distance and close-relative avoidance, imports and foreign AI sires
  registered with short export pedigrees, litters with inbreeding depression, deaths.
- Genetics: neutral gene drop for realised IBD, five recessive disease loci with DNA testing
  that ramps up after a test becomes available, a polygenic hip-dysplasia liability trait with
  FCI A to E screening, and EBVs from a registry-only pedigree BLUP.
- Run `20261011-default-s42`: seed 42, config hash `68d35948916204bde48c1c50ab64ca7cef465c01961821f0dd3aac318cd0a72a`, git commit
  `6c2d30af31fb3c1078e0a4932f8fb60aa3d23903`, package version 0.1.0,
  generation time 70.61 s. The exact configs are in
  `config_snapshot/`.

## Known deviations and limitations
- HD is analysed with a linear model on grade scores; variance components are the simulation's
  true values, not estimated.
- No parentage errors, no recording errors, exact DNA tests.
- Disease allele frequencies are illustrative assumptions (not calibrated to real breeds).
- Neutral and disease loci are unlinked; realised IBD is measured on 2,000 unlinked loci.
- At most one litter per dam per year; registration, import and AI processes are stylised.
- Recorded pedigrees of imports can grow when their ancestors were already registered
  (first registration wins).
- Breed archetypes are calibrated to published distributions (for example Ne from Lewis et al.
  2015), not to any real breed. SYN_S is very inbred (realised IBD about 0.43 in 2025).
- Dates run to the end of 2026; the EBV evaluation date is 2026-09-30.

## Uses
- **Intended:** benchmarking pedigree, kinship and inbreeding algorithms against ground truth;
  testing mate-matching and rule-compliance tools; teaching; prototyping registry software.
- **Out of scope:** decisions about real animals; estimates of real breed parameters; any
  claim about real breeds.
