# Breeding Smarter: partner with us

*University of Zagreb, Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computing. BioMedVetMech 2026.*

## The problem
Purebred dogs carry high inbreeding (mean genomic inbreeding {lit_genomic_f} across
{lit_breeds_genotyped} breeds), popular sires dominate breeding in every Kennel Club breed, and
{lit_carrier_share} of screened dogs carry a disease variant. EU Regulation 2026/1818 now bans
close-relative matings across the EU. Clubs have lookup tools, not decision tools.

## What we have
- An open pedigree engine: exact inbreeding, kinship and diversity, {bench_inbreeding_250k_s} s
  for 250,000 dogs.
- A rule engine with EU 2026/1818, FCI, Kennel Club, Raad van Beheer and HKS profiles.
- An explained mate recommender: predicted COI with the ancestors that cause it, litter
  diversity, DNA-based disease risk, hip-dysplasia breeding values, sire use, and rule checks.
- A validated synthetic benchmark ({n_animals} synthetic dogs) and a live demo.

## What we offer a partner (free pilot)
- A diversity report for one breed (inbreeding trends, Ne, founders, sire concentration).
- A compliance check of planned matings against EU 2026/1818 and your own rules.
- Mate recommendations with score cards for your breeders.
- A club season plan: litter quotas per sire that keep long-term diversity, with breeders free
  to choose within them.

## What we need
Pedigree, health and DNA data under a data-sharing agreement, or an **on-premises run**: the
code goes to the data and nothing leaves the club.

## Honest about limits
Results so far are on synthetic data. Our simulations show that rankings help with disease and
short-term inbreeding, and that club quotas on sire use raise long-term diversity (Ne
{p12_ne_S_s1} to {p12_ne_S_cc_ocs_mc1} in a small breed): we want to test this with you on
real breeds.
